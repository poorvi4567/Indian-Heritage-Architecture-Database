About This Project

This platform serves as a centralized digital repository of India's rich architectural heritage and historical monuments. Our mission is to make information about India's cultural treasures accessible, searchable, and comprehensive.

By combining structured relational data with flexible document storage, we provide detailed information on thousands of monuments including their history, architectural styles, visitor information, and cultural significance.

Technology Stack

  • Backend: Django (Python)
  • Relational Database: SQLite (monuments, locations, styles, periods)
  • NoSQL Database: MongoDB Atlas (visitor info, reviews, images, descriptions)
  • Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript
  • Mapping: Leaflet.js
